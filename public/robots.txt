# The guest entry points are per-hotel URLs (/h/<slug>) that a guest reaches by
# scanning a printed QR code in their room. There is nothing for a search engine
# to usefully index there, and two reasons not to.
#
# On a demo instance, several of the seeded hotels are real businesses being
# approached as pilot candidates (see db/seeds/demo/hotels/). Their pages carry
# the hotel's own name and a knowledge base that is only partly their published
# information — fine in a sales conversation where everyone knows what they are
# looking at, misleading if a guest of that hotel found it through a search
# result instead.
#
# On a real installation, a hotel's guest chat is not a page it wants ranking
# against its own website.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /h/
Disallow: /guest/
